Dengue in Rio de Janeiro state, Brazil, 2024
============================================

Data:     Sánchez-Marqués R, Sánchez JS. DATASET_MULTIMODAL_V8: A Nationwide
          Multimodal Dataset for Dengue Surveillance, Nowcasting and
          Forecasting in Brazil (2010–2025). Zenodo, 2026.
          doi:10.5281/zenodo.22029053
          Its case counts come from InfoDengue (https://info.dengue.mat.br/).

Terms:    Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International, as stated on the
          Zenodo record. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
          The licence text is in LICENSE-data.

Changes:  data/dengue-rj-2024.csv keeps the rows of dataset_multimodal_v8.parquet
          with estado RJ and anio 2024, and 18 of its 87 columns, as published:
          nothing renamed, recoded, deduplicated or filled. data_iniSE is
          written as a date. Rows are sorted by municipality and week.

Boundaries: geoBoundaries gbOpen BRA ADM2, release 9469f09, from IBGE via
          OCHA ROLAC, 2020. Runfola D, et al. (2020) geoBoundaries: A global
          database of political administrative boundaries. PLoS ONE 15(4):
          e0231866. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0231866
          Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 IGO.
          https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/
          Changes: the 92 municipalities of Rio de Janeiro state, matched to the
          dataset's IBGE codes by name; coordinates rounded to 6 decimals.
